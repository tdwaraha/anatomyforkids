Privacy Policy — Human Anatomy for Kids

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Human Anatomy for Kids is an educational app for children, developed by Dwaraha Thushaanth. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with information.

Short version

The app collects no data at all. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking, and no network services. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, location data, photos, or contacts — from adults or from children.

Children's privacy

This app is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children either. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no external links, and no way for a child to share personal information through the app.

On-device storage

Exploration stars and settings (sound effects, haptics, voice, and music) are stored only on your device using the device's standard storage. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Third-party services

None. The app uses no third-party software kits for advertising, analytics, or tracking. Read-aloud narration uses Apple's built-in system voices, processed on your device.

Sharing

The app does not send any data to us or to any third party. The snapshot feature in the Realistic 3D viewer only shares an image through the device's own share sheet, to a destination you choose yourself.

Content credits

Realistic 3D organ models are derived from BodyParts3D polygon data, © Database Center for Life Science, licensed under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan. The models are simplified educational illustrations, not a clinical anatomy atlas or medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
