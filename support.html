Support — Human Anatomy for Kids

Human Anatomy for Kids turns learning about the body into a hands-on adventure: 36 organs and body structures across 11 body systems, in two modes — a friendly Kids Learning explorer and a Realistic 3D viewer — plus a Body Detective quiz and an Organ Match game.

Getting started

  • Open the app and tap any part of the body, or use the search and system buttons to pick an organ.
  • Kids Learning shows the cartoon body with read-aloud explanations, fun facts, and stars to collect.
  • Realistic 3D shows detailed 3D organ models you can rotate, zoom, and inspect, with labels and key structures.
  • Tap the info button (top right) to open the "For grown-ups" sheet, where you can change sound, haptics, voice, and music settings.

Good to know

  • The app works fully offline — no internet needed, no account, no purchases.
  • Stars and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account, so progress cannot be transferred to another device. Deleting the app erases it.
  • The models are simplified educational illustrations, not a clinical anatomy atlas or medical advice.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound? The app's sound effects respect the silent switch — turn the silent switch off. Narration and sound settings are in the "For grown-ups" sheet (info button, top right).
  • Something looks wrong? Try closing the app fully and reopening it. If a model still looks off, send us a screenshot (see Contact below).
  • Lost your stars? Stars live only on the device where they were earned. Reinstalling the app or moving to a new device starts fresh.

Contact

Questions, or found a problem? Email us at Dwaraha.rpht@outlook.com. It helps if you include your device model, iOS version, and a screenshot of what you see.
